Short Description for Submission

MediBridge is a borderless healthcare coordination platform designed to connect patients suffering from rare and complex diseases with the right specialists, hospitals, and research ecosystems regardless of geography. This MVP demonstrates the end-to-end patient journey—from patient onboarding and case triage to specialist matching, virtual consultation, and local treatment coordination. Through a smart routing workflow, MediBridge reduces delays in diagnosis, expands access to expert care, and simplifies cross-border medical collaboration.

How to Demo to Judges
