# MediBridge

## Borderless Healthcare Coordination Platform

MediBridge is an AI-powered healthcare coordination platform designed to simplify and accelerate the patient journey for individuals suffering from rare and complex medical conditions.

The platform connects patients with the right specialists, hospitals, and treatment pathways regardless of geographical boundaries.

Built as part of the Forage India National Buildathon, MediBridge focuses on reducing delays in diagnosis, improving accessibility to expert healthcare, and enabling seamless medical coordination.

---

## Problem Statement

Patients suffering from rare or complex diseases often face:

- Delayed diagnosis
- Fragmented healthcare systems
- Difficulty finding the right specialists
- Poor coordination between hospitals
- Limited access to cross-border healthcare expertise

---

## Solution

MediBridge creates an intelligent healthcare navigation system that supports the complete patient journey through:

- Patient onboarding
- Smart case triage
- Specialist matching
- Consultation booking
- Local hospital coordination
- Treatment pathway tracking

---

## Core Features

### 1. Patient Onboarding

Patients can enter key details such as:

- Name
- Age
- Symptoms
- Disease category
- Location
- Medical history

### 2. Smart Case Triage

The system simulates:

- Severity scoring
- Priority level detection
- Recommended medical specialty
- Cross-border care suggestion

### 3. Specialist Matching

Patients receive specialist recommendations based on:

- Medical condition
- Required specialty
- Location preference
- Match percentage

### 4. Consultation Booking

The MVP allows patients to:

- Select a specialist
- Choose consultation timing
- Confirm appointment details

### 5. Treatment Coordination Dashboard

Patients can view:

- Hospital referral status
- Treatment pathway
- Follow-up actions
- Care progress
